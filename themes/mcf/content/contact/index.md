## How to reach us

{{< cards >}}
{{< card title="Prospective students" icon="lightbulb" >}}
Interested in a PhD, masters, honours or visiting position? Read [Join Us](/join/) for current opportunities and what to include in your email, then contact us at [t.mitchell@uq.edu.au](mailto:t.mitchell@uq.edu.au).
{{< /card >}}
{{< card title="Industry and research collaboration" icon="comment" >}}
We work with industry, government and academic partners on modelling challenges in the energy and resources sector. Send a short description of the problem and what you hope to achieve; see our [research themes](/research/) for the areas we cover.
{{< /card >}}
{{< card title="Media" icon="circle-info" >}}
For comment on our research, email the group with the topic, the outlet and your deadline, and we will respond as quickly as we can.
{{< /card >}}
{{< /cards >}}
