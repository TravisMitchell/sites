Associate Professor Christopher Leonardi co-founded the Modelling Complex Fluids (MCF) group at The University of Queensland in 2016 with [Dr Travis Mitchell](/people/t-mitchell/).

His research centres on the computational modelling of complex fluid systems, with a particular focus on the lattice Boltzmann method and its coupling with the discrete element method to simulate particle suspensions and fluid–structure interaction. Applications of this work include multiphase and non-Newtonian flows, flow and particle transport in porous and fractured media (such as proppant transport in hydraulic fracturing of coal seam gas reservoirs), heat transfer, and airflow and methane dispersion in underground mine ventilation.
